Studio Policies & Guidelines

  • Clients must be at least 18 years old (valid ID required on studio arrival).
  • No Alcohol or Blood-thinners: Avoid consuming alcohol or aspirin 24 hours prior to your session.
  • Eat & Hydrate: Ensure you eat a full meal and stay hydrated before coming to the home studio.
  • Deposits: Deposits are strictly non-refundable but transferable if rescheduled at least 48 hours in advance.
